BEATREEL - QUICK INSTALL (WINDOWS 10/11, 64-bit)
=================================================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. your Desktop).
2. Double-click  Install-BEATREEL.bat  and allow it to make changes.
   It copies:
     BEATREEL.vst3  ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
     ffmpeg.exe     ->  %APPDATA%\BEATREEL\         (encodes the MP4)
     BEATREEL.exe   ->  C:\Program Files\BEATREEL\  (standalone app)
3. Open your DAW and rescan plugins.
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins, then star BEATREEL.
4. Put BEATREEL LAST on your master track.

Manual install: copy the BEATREEL.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
and ffmpeg.exe into %APPDATA%\BEATREEL (paste that path into Explorer's address bar).

MAKE A REEL
  1. Select the loop / region you want in the playlist.
  2. Press REC in BEATREEL, then press play. It stops by itself at the end of the
     loop (or press REC again). Rendering your song with REC armed also works.
  3. LOAD .MID - export the MIDI of your melody and load it for the piano roll.
  4. Pick one of the 12 looks or hit RANDOMIZE, then make it yours in STYLE
     (colours, background, image, fonts, your own .ttf/.otf fonts).
  5. Choose 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9 and hit EXPORT. The MP4 carries your original audio.

ACTIVATE
  Click ACTIVATE and paste the key from your purchase email. No internet needed.
  One key per purchase, for up to 3 of your computers (Windows or Mac).
  Without a key, exports carry a "DEMO" watermark.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  * "ffmpeg not found": INFO tab > LOCATE FFMPEG, and pick ffmpeg.exe.
  * Plugin not showing up: make sure your DAW is 64-bit and scans
    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  * Your presets and captures live in %APPDATA%\BEATREEL (INFO > OPEN FOLDER).
