BEATREEL - QUICK INSTALL (macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon & Intel)
===================================================================

1. Unzip BEATREEL-macOS.zip.
2. Right-click  "Install BEATREEL.command"  > Open  (the first time macOS asks
   whether you trust it - click Open).
   It copies:
     BEATREEL.vst3       -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
     BEATREEL.component  -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/   (Audio Unit)
     BEATREEL.app        -> ~/Applications/                          (standalone)
     ffmpeg              -> ~/Library/Application Support/BEATREEL/  (encodes the MP4)
3. Restart your DAW and put BEATREEL LAST on your master track.
   Logic / GarageBand use the AU; FL Studio, Ableton, Reaper, Studio One etc. use the VST3.

No ffmpeg in the zip? Install it once with Homebrew:  brew install ffmpeg
(BEATREEL finds it automatically.)

MAKE A REEL
  1. Select the loop / region you want.
  2. Press REC in BEATREEL, then press play. It stops by itself at the end of the
     loop (or press REC again). Bouncing your song with REC armed also works.
  3. LOAD .MID - load the MIDI of your melody for the piano roll.
  4. Pick one of the 12 looks or hit RANDOMIZE, then tweak it in STYLE.
  5. Choose 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9 and hit EXPORT. The MP4 carries your original audio.

ACTIVATE
  Click ACTIVATE and paste the key from your purchase email. No internet needed.
  One key per purchase, for up to 3 of your computers (Windows or Mac).
  Without a key, exports carry a "DEMO" watermark.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  * "BEATREEL is damaged / can't be opened": run the installer again (it clears
    the download quarantine), or in Terminal:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/BEATREEL.vst3
  * AU not showing in Logic: restart the Mac once, or run the installer again.
  * Presets and captures: ~/Library/Application Support/BEATREEL (INFO > OPEN FOLDER).
